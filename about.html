About Car Automation

Car Automation is an automotive content creation and publishing platform designed for creators who want to manage their car-related video content from one place.

Our Platform

The platform is designed to help users prepare automotive videos, create captions and hashtags, connect an authorized TikTok account, and publish content using supported TikTok services.

Our Goal

Our goal is to simplify automotive content creation and provide creators with an easy-to-use workflow for managing their videos.

TikTok Integration

TikTok integration requires user authorization. Users remain in control of their TikTok account and the content they choose to publish.
