Car Automation respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our automotive content creation and publishing platform.

When you connect your TikTok account, Car Automation may receive information that is authorized through TikTok's authentication system. This may include information required to identify your authorized TikTok account and publish content.

Car Automation uses TikTok's official authorization system. Users must authorize access before the application can perform supported actions on their TikTok account.

3. Video Content

Users may provide automotive videos, captions and hashtags for publishing. Content is used only for the functionality provided by the application.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the application. Access credentials and authorization information should not be shared with other users.

5. Third-Party Services

The application may interact with TikTok services and APIs. Your use of TikTok is also subject to TikTok's own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary to provide the application's functionality and maintain authorized integrations.

7. User Control

Users remain in control of their TikTok account and content. Authorization can be managed through the relevant account and service settings.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the application owner through the contact information provided with the application.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its services change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
